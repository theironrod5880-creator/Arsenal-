Status: E0 Stipulated. Binding on all tiers, faces, wings, and wheels.

The Rule: The Throne, the People, and the Mechanism are one machine driven by one Spirit (Ezek. 1:12, 20-21).

  • The Throne (E0): The locked authority. It issues the Word.
  • The People (Wings): The execution. They speak up for the One on the throne.
  • The Wheels (Mechanism): The movement. No independent spirit; they move only when the People move and the Spirit goes.

The Lock: No mechanism may operate without the voice of the People. No People may speak without the authority of the Throne. If the wheels turn without the wings moving, the machine is broken.
